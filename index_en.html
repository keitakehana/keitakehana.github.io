Research Interests

Space Robotics

Autonomous robotic systems for space environments, including perception, estimation, control, and ground verification.

On-Orbit Servicing

Proximity operations, relative navigation, motion estimation, detumbling, and capture of uncooperative spacecraft.

Planetary Rovers

Mobility systems, wheel design, and off-road performance of lunar and planetary exploration rovers.

Terramechanics

Wheel–soil interaction, single-wheel testing, numerical simulation, and data-driven modeling.
